This document lists the trademarks, service marks, and brand assets used by
UIAI Engine and WPUIAI / Startempire Wire, and the rules for using them.

## Marks

The following names and marks are trademarks or service marks of WPUIAI /
Startempire Wire:

- UIAI Engine
- WPUIAI
- Startempire Wire
- Focusa (where the mark is used by WPUIAI / Startempire Wire for UIAI Engine
  integration or co-marketing purposes)
- the UIAI Engine logomark
- the Focusa logomark (where used by WPUIAI / Startempire Wire)

Visual assets are stored under `assets/branding/`. Do not modify, recolor, skew,
or stretch the marks. Do not place the marks on a background that materially
reduces legibility.

## Use without permission

You may use the marks without prior written permission in the following cases:

- Identifying UIAI Engine or WPUIAI / Startempire Wire in plain text, including
  documentation, articles, talks, and screenshots.
- Linking to the UIAI Engine repository or to engine.focusa.dev.
- Indicating that your product integrates with UIAI Engine, provided the
  statement is truthful and the marks are not modified.
- Internal evaluation, development, and testing of UIAI Engine on your own
  systems.

## Use that requires permission

You need written permission to use the marks:

- In the name of a product, service, company, or domain that competes with
  WPUIAI / Startempire Wire or that could be confused with it.
- In marketing or advertising in a way that suggests endorsement, sponsorship,
  or partnership that does not exist.
- On merchandise, physical goods, or paid promotional materials.
- In any way that is false, misleading, defamatory, or disparaging.

## Open source and BSL considerations

The BSL in `LICENSE.md` does not grant trademark rights. The Additional Use
Grant covers the use of the source code for personal, educational, evaluation,
and non-commercial purposes, but does not authorize use of the marks in a way
that suggests endorsement.

If you fork UIAI Engine under the Change License terms after the Change Date,
remove the WPUIAI / Startempire Wire marks from your fork and pick a different
name to avoid confusion with the original project.

## Reporting misuse

If you believe the marks are being used in a way that violates this policy,
email `legal@startempirewire.com` with:

- the URL, listing, screenshot, or other location of the use
- a short description of the concern
- your contact details

WPUIAI / Startempire Wire reviews concerns on a case-by-case basis and
responds where the use is misleading or commercially harmful.
