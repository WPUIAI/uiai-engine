## Claim-substantiation addendum — proving “most extensive and valid”

The system must not market itself as the most extensive/valid proof-of-agent-work system merely because the specification is long or the UI is polished. Add the following requirements; comprehensive gap register total: **150**.

**EPWA-GAP-141 — Validity-layer model.** Formalize separate integrity, provenance, observation, evidentiary-sufficiency, verification, completion, settlement, and legal-admissibility states. Never collapse them into one green “valid” badge.

**EPWA-GAP-142 — Terminology.** Distinguish verifiable agent-work evidence/receipts from blockchain consensus “proof of work,” employment proof, legal evidence, and cryptographic attestation. Public claims must use precise language.

**EPWA-GAP-143 — Standards map.** Produce an applicability/conformance matrix against relevant open standards and practices—such as W3C PROV, in-toto/DSSE/Sigstore/SLSA provenance, C2PA media provenance, SPDX/CycloneDX where software artifacts apply, CloudEvents/OpenTelemetry, applicable verifiable-credential/signature approaches, WCAG/PDF-UA/PDF-A, and evolving transparency-log standards. “Mapped” is not “conformant.”

**EPWA-GAP-144 — Competitive landscape benchmark.** Define a dated, reproducible benchmark cohort across agent observability/evaluation, CI/CD attestations, software supply-chain provenance, browser automation evidence, audit/compliance workpapers, RPA/process mining, test reporting, incident evidence, and collaborative work-report systems.

**EPWA-GAP-145 — Public conformance corpus.** Publish vendor-neutral fixtures covering valid, partial, missing, stale, contradictory, forged, scope-mismatched, cherry-picked, redacted, corrupt, revoked, imported, offline, and adversarial artifacts, plus expected machine decisions and human rendering.

**EPWA-GAP-146 — Comparative metrics.** Measure schema/authority coverage, false-done prevention, false-pass/false-fail rates, missing-evidence detection, redaction leakage, provenance integrity, cross-instance portability, recovery, accessibility, agent token efficiency, human decision quality/time, latency, storage, and cost per verified outcome.

**EPWA-GAP-147 — Independent assessment.** Require external security review, privacy review, accessibility audit, cryptographic/provenance review, LLM-judge calibration review, and independent reproduction of installed end-to-end claims. Internal dogfood alone cannot establish superiority.

**EPWA-GAP-148 — Interoperability event.** Prove import/export and verification across independent implementations/instances/harnesses, not only two copies of the same code. Record deviations and unsupported semantics.

**EPWA-GAP-149 — Public claim evidence packet.** Every superlative public claim must link to a dated methodology, cohort, versions, exclusions, raw results, independent reviewers, uncertainty, known weaknesses, and expiry/revalidation date. Narrow claims are preferred over universal claims.

**EPWA-GAP-150 — Claim governance.** Define approval authority, legal/marketing review, challenge/correction process, automatic expiry after material version/benchmark changes, and immediate withdrawal when contrary evidence appears.

### Truthful claim ladder

Before independent benchmarking:

> Focusa provides a comprehensive, Project/Workstream-scoped evidence and completion architecture for agent work.

After complete installed IR6 proof:

> Focusa provides an extensively verified end-to-end agent-work evidence, verification, and closure system across the tested capabilities and environments.

Only after EPWA-GAP-143–150 pass against a current representative cohort:

> Focusa is the most comprehensive system measured by the published benchmark dimensions and cohort as of the stated date.

Never claim universal “most valid.” Validity is claim-, scope-, policy-, evidence-, verifier-, time-, and jurisdiction-dependent.
